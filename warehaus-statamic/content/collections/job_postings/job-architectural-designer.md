overview: >-
  At Warehaus, our architectural team works across a diverse range of projects, from large-scale commercial and industrial facilities to renovations and adaptive reuse. We're looking for an experienced
  Architectural Designer who can contribute to both design development and technical documentation. If you enjoy turning design concepts into detailed, buildable solutions—and want to stay involved from
  early design through construction—this is a role where you'll stay engaged. This position focuses on developing architectural drawings, models, and construction documents while working closely with
  project architects, managers, and engineers. You'll be involved throughout the design process, from schematic design through construction administration, with opportunities to contribute ideas, solve
  technical challenges, and take ownership of portions of the project.
responsibilities:
  - Develop architectural drawings and construction documents from schematic design through construction documentation
  - Prepare models, renderings, diagrams, and presentation materials to communicate design concepts
  - Translate design intent into accurate, coordinated construction documents
  - Apply knowledge of building materials, construction methods, codes, and regulatory requirements
  - Participate in design discussions, charrettes, and project coordination meetings
  - Coordinate with architects, engineers, consultants, and other project team members
  - Support construction administration, including RFIs, submittals, shop drawing reviews, and punch lists
  - Assist with permitting and regulatory approval processes
  - Maintain organized, accurate project files and drawing sets throughout each phase
  - Manage assigned design and production tasks within project schedules and budgets
qualifications:
  - 3-5 years of professional architectural experience working on commercial, industrial, institutional, renovation, or adaptive reuse projects
  - Proficiency in Revit and AutoCAD
  - Proficiency in architectural detailing, design development, and construction documentation
  - Strong understanding of building materials, construction methods, and applicable building codes
  - Experience with construction administration processes, including RFIs, submittals, shop drawings, and punch lists
  - Familiarity with local and state permitting and regulatory approval processes
  - Ability to communicate design intent and coordinate effectively with project teams and consultants
  - Strong attention to detail, organizational skills, and problem-solving abilities
  - Ability to work within a collaborative, multidisciplinary team
  - Architectural registration in Pennsylvania and/or LEED accreditation are a plus, but not required
