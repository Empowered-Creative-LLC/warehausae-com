  **YORK, PA – October 29, 2026** - Warehaus, a multidisciplinary architecture, engineering, and design firm headquartered in York, Pennsylvania, announces the addition of Brian Welsh as Business Development Manager. In this role, Welsh will lead efforts to expand the firm's client relationships, identify new business opportunities, and support strategic growth throughout Central Pennsylvania and the Mid-Atlantic region.

  Welsh brings more than 20 years of experience in business development, strategic account management, marketing, and client relations. Most recently, he spent 13 years with Fenner Drives, a division of Fenner Precision Polymers and part of the Michelin Group, where he held progressively responsible roles in market development, marketing communications, and business development. His experience includes managing key accounts, developing market strategies, and building long-term client relationships that drive measurable business growth.

  Earlier in his career, Welsh served as Client Services Manager at RETTEW Associates, a Lancaster-based engineering and consulting firm, providing him with direct experience in the architecture, engineering, and consulting industry.

  As Business Development Manager, Welsh will work closely with Warehaus leadership, project directors, and the marketing team to strengthen the firm's market presence, cultivate relationships with prospective clients, and align business development initiatives with the firm's architectural and engineering expertise. His responsibilities will also include market research, strategic prospecting, and identifying opportunities across Warehaus' core markets.

  "Brian brings a valuable combination of relationship-building experience, market intelligence, and strategic thinking to our team," said Lauren Bergman, Chief Brand & Culture Officer at Warehaus. "As we continue to strengthen our business development efforts, his ability to connect client needs with meaningful solutions will help us build new relationships, deepen existing partnerships, and pursue opportunities that align with our long-term growth strategy."

  Welsh earned a Bachelor of Arts in English with a concentration in Communications from Lebanon Valley College.

  **About Warehaus**

  Warehaus is a multidisciplinary architecture, engineering, and design firm headquartered in York, Pennsylvania. The firm provides architecture, civil engineering, structural engineering, interior design, and historic preservation services to clients across the Mid-Atlantic region. Guided by its philosophy, We Listen – We Design – We Deliver, Warehaus develops thoughtful, collaborative solutions that respond to each client's unique goals.

  For more information, visit www.warehausae.com.
