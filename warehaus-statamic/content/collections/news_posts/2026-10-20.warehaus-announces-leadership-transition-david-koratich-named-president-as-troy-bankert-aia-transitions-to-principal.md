  **York, PA – October 20, 2026**

  Warehaus announced today that David Koratich has been named President of the firm, succeeding longtime President and CEO Troy Bankert, AIA, who will transition to the role of Principal.

  Koratich, who currently serves as Chief Operating Officer, will take responsibility for the day-to-day leadership and operations of Warehaus. His responsibilities as COO will be incorporated into his role as President. Bankert and Koratich will both remain co-owners of the firm.

  The transition marks the beginning of a new leadership structure for Warehaus, with Koratich assuming primary responsibility for the day-to-day leadership and operations of the firm. Bankert will remain an active member of the firm’s executive leadership team, working alongside Koratich to help shape Warehaus’ long-term strategy, growth and future direction.

  As Principal, Bankert will focus more of his time on client relationships, architectural pursuits and business development. He will continue to lead client outreach, represent Warehaus in architectural opportunities and work closely with the firm’s internal business development team to maintain existing relationships and develop new opportunities.

  The transition builds on a partnership that has developed over decades. Bankert has spent 38 years with Warehaus, advancing from designer to President and CEO and helping guide the growth of the firm throughout his career. Koratich has been with Warehaus for 27 years and, as COO, has already been closely involved in the firm’s operations, leadership and long-term planning.

  “Troy and I have worked side by side for many years, and we’ve always shared the same commitment to Warehaus and the same vision for its future,” said Koratich. “I’m looking forward to taking on the day-to-day leadership of the firm while continuing to work closely with Troy on our long-term direction. His relationships with our clients and his ability to create opportunities for Warehaus are tremendous strengths, and this structure allows both of us to focus our time where we can make the biggest impact.”

  Bankert said the transition is a natural next step in their longstanding partnership. “David and I have been in lockstep for a long time, and I have complete confidence in him leading the firm,” said Bankert. “He has already been handling much of the day-to-day leadership of Warehaus through his role as COO, so this is a logical progression for us. It also gives me the opportunity to spend more time with our clients, pursue new architectural opportunities and focus on business development, which are areas of the business I’ve always enjoyed.”

  Both Bankert and Koratich will remain actively involved in shaping Warehaus’ future as co-owners and members of the firm’s executive leadership team.
