  **YORK, PA — October 21, 2026**


  Warehaus, a York-based architecture, engineering, and design firm, announced that David Koratich has been named president of the firm, succeeding longtime president and CEO Troy Bankert, AIA, who will transition to the role of principal.


  Koratich, who serves as Chief Operating Officer, will take responsibility for the day-to-day leadership and operations of Warehaus. His responsibilities as COO will be incorporated into his role as president. Bankert and Koratich will remain co-owners of the firm.


  "Troy and I have worked side by side for many years, and we've always shared the same commitment to Warehaus and the same vision for its future," Koratich said. "I'm looking forward to taking on the day-to-day leadership of the firm while continuing to work closely with Troy on our long-term direction. His relationships with our clients and his ability to create opportunities for Warehaus are tremendous strengths, and this structure allows both of us to focus our time where we can make the biggest impact."


  Koratich assumes primary responsibility for the day-to-day leadership and operations of the firm. Bankert will remain an active member of the firm's executive leadership team, working alongside Koratich to help shape Warehaus' long-term strategy, growth and future direction.


  Bankert will focus more on client relationships, architectural pursuits, and business development. He will continue to lead client outreach, represent Warehaus in architectural opportunities, and work with the firm's internal business development team to maintain relationships and develop new opportunities.


  Bankert has spent 38 years with Warehaus, advancing from designer to president and CEO and helping guide the growth of the firm throughout his career. Koratich has been with Warehaus for 27 years and, as COO, has been involved in the firm's operations, leadership, and long-term planning.


  "David and I have been in lockstep for a long time, and I have complete confidence in him leading the firm," said Bankert. "He has already been handling much of the day-to-day leadership of Warehaus through his role as COO, so this is a logical progression for us. It also gives me the opportunity to spend more time with our clients, pursue new architectural opportunities and focus on business development, which are areas of the business I've always enjoyed."


  Bankert and Koratich will serve as co-owners and members of the firm's executive leadership team.
